I feel like you missed an opportunity to talk more about the free Stripe for Salesforce app developed and maintained by Stripe (also known as Stripe Universal Connector). It can integrate with multiple Stripe accounts and populate custom objects (Stripe Events) from configurable webhooks, which can then be used downstream by Apex or Flow.
